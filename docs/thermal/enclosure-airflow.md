# Enclosure Thermal Considerations

## Passive Cooling
[DESIGN CHOICE] Passive cooling mechanisms include:
- Copper spreading across inner and outer PCB layers.
- Thermal vias moving heat from components to opposite-side copper planes.
- Enclosure conduction.
- Natural convection within the enclosure.

## Active Cooling
[DESIGN CHOICE] Active cooling via fans or forced airflow is TBD.

## Tradeoffs
[THEORY] The decision between passive and active cooling involves tradeoffs:
- **Noise:** Active is louder.
- **Reliability:** Fans introduce mechanical failure points.
- **Dust/Ingress:** Active airflow pulls in dust unless filtered.
- **Power:** Fans consume system power budget.
- **Size:** Fans and larger heatsinks increase volume.
- **Maintenance:** Filters and fans require periodic service.
