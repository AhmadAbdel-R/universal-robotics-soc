## Why Selected

**[DESIGN CHOICE]** The STM32MP257 was selected because:

1. **World-class public documentation** — no NDAs, no CDN blocks, massive ST Wiki
2. **Robotics-optimized feature set** — NPU, M33 real-time, 3× CAN-FD, dual GbE, PCIe Gen2
3. **Economic BOM** — lower total system cost than NXP alternatives
4. **Proven ecosystem** — STM32CubeMX, CubeProgrammer, CubeIDE, OpenSTLinux
5. **Industrial longevity** — ST commits to long production lifetimes

See [Decision Log ADR-003](../decisions/decision-log.md#adr-003---final-pivot-to-stmicroelectronics-stm32mp257).
